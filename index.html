About the Clinic

Calm, unhurried eye care you can trust

Vasuki Eye Care is a neighbourhood eye clinic on 8th Main Road, Koramangala, run by Dr. Sunitha P. Patients come here for accurate diagnosis, clear explanations and a comfortable experience — from a routine eye test to cataract and glaucoma surgery.

The clinic is built around a simple idea: you should understand your own eyes before you agree to any treatment. Consultations are deliberately unhurried, findings are shown to you on the screen, and every option — including the option to wait — is explained in plain language before anything is decided.

Comprehensive Eye Exams

Painless Cataract Surgery
